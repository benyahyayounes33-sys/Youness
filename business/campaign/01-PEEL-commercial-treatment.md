## 0. Read this first: how to make it look expensive for real

**Why I did not generate the final film.** Your rule #8 is right: the poster must not be invented. Today's AI video generators (Kling, Runway, Veo) redraw every frame. On a moving poster they **warp lines, letters and details** 🔶. That would be false advertising for a print brand. And I don't have your logo or photos of the real printed adhesive poster yet.

**The method that gives a premium result:** **real footage wherever the poster is visible**, filmed with a phone, a lamp and black card. **AI only for atmosphere** (an empty room, light, haze) where the poster is unreadable, or as a backup in image-to-video mode from your own photo, checked frame by frame.

| Shot | Method | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 macro ink, S2 corner, S3 peel, S5 contact, S6 smoothing | **REAL** (phone + macro) | Real texture and real hands are impossible to fake convincingly. AI deforms hands and paper. |
| S4 travel to the wall, S9 arc, S10 desire | **REAL** (AI as backup with your photo as the first frame) | The poster is visible. |
| S7 darkness | REAL or AI | Poster not readable. |
| S8 room reveal | REAL, or **AI room + your real poster composited** (corner pin) | If your room isn't "premium" enough, AI builds the set. |
| S11 brand | **Motion graphics** with your real logo file | No AI: no deformed logo. |

**Never write or show in the ad** ⚠️ (unless tested and confirmed): removable, repositionable, damage-free, waterproof, washable, "museum quality", "lasts forever". So **don't show**: peeling the poster off the wall, applying it to bathroom tiles or a wet surface, re-sticking it. The text in this treatment makes no promises: it describes gestures (peel, place), not performance.

**Copies needed:** each peel take uses up one poster. Plan **3 adhesive A4 prints** of the hero poster (get the price from your shop).

## 1. Three concepts

### Concept A: “PEEL” ✅ recommended
**Logline:** the ritual of installing a poster, filmed like the unveiling of a luxury watch.
**Arc:** HOOK (macro of ink in raking light + corner lift, 0–2 s) → REVEAL (the peel) → EXPERIENCE (placing, smoothing) → **1 s of silence in the dark** → DESIRE (the lamp clicks, the room comes alive) → BRAND (the logo "peels" onto the screen).
**Why it works:** the hook is sensory (sight + ASMR peel sound), so the brain stops scrolling 🔶. The product is the hero in every shot. The **peel sound becomes your sonic logo**, reusable in every future ad.
**Difficulty:** low. Shot in a dark corner of a room, half a day, with no studio.

### Concept B: “SILENT WALL”
**Logline:** "A blank wall says nothing about you."
**Arc:** a dark, empty, sad room → a student comes in, takes out the poster → peel, install → the light comes up and the room "speaks" → line: *Votre mur. Votre histoire.*
**Strength:** emotion and identity, very strong for students. **Limits:** needs a convincing actor and room; 18 s is short for a character to exist.

### Concept C: “RHYMES” (collection)
**Logline:** the world rhymes with your posters.
**Arc:** match cuts between real-life shapes and the artwork: a wave in a glass of water → *The Great Wave*; city lights at night → *The Starry Night*; a reflection of gold → *The Kiss*. Final shot: all three posters on one wall.
**Strength:** sells the **collection** (bigger basket, bundles). **Limits:** demanding to shoot, and each poster appears only briefly.

**Choice:** **A** for the launch ad (simple, product-accurate, strong hook). B as the second ad if A performs. C when the collection has more than 6 designs.

## 3. Shot-by-shot cinematography plan

### 3.1 Kit and settings (≈ 0 MAD beyond the prints) 🔶
- **Camera:** your phone, **4K 24 fps**. If possible use a free app with manual settings (e.g. Blackmagic Camera, iOS and Android ✅). Shutter **1/50 s** at 24 fps (180° rule), **1/250 s** in slow motion. **Lock exposure and focus** on every shot.
- **Slow motion:** 120 fps if available (60 fps minimum). On many phones slow motion is in 1080p, not 4K 🔶. That's acceptable on macro shots with soft backgrounds.
- **Log profile** if your phone has one (e.g. Apple Log on recent iPhones ✅). Otherwise standard profile, exposed for the highlights.
- **Light:** 1 desk lamp (warm bulb) + 1 LED torch. **Black card** to block spill ("negative fill"), **white card** to bounce, baking paper to diffuse, a **cardboard tube** to turn the lamp into a spotlight (snoot).
- **Background:** a **matte**, dark wall, or a large matte black / charcoal foam board (from a stationery shop) as a "wall" for close shots. No gloss, no busy patterns.
- **Stabilisation:** tripod + phone slid on a cloth across a table (a homemade slider) for slow movements.
- **Hands:** clean, short natural nails, no visible jewellery, dark long sleeve.
- ⚠️ **No visible brands** (headphones, laptop, controller, phone): turn them around or cover the logo with black tape. On the **liner**, film a blank area (some liners carry the paper maker's printed brand).

### 3.2 Shot by shot
| # | Lens (look) | Framing and movement | Speed | Focus | Light | Practical tips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 100 mm macro (phone macro mode) | The wave crest fills the frame; 2–3 mm lateral slide | 24 fps | Fixed on the ink | **Hard raking light** at a very low angle, moved by hand from left to right; everything else black | Do 10 passes of the light, keep the best one. Show the **real paper texture**. |
| S2 | 100 mm macro | Static. The poster face down on black; the corner of the liner lifts | 120 fps → 20 % | On the corner edge | Soft top-left key + edge highlight | The lift must take ≈ 0.25 s in real time to fill 1 s on screen. |
| S3 | 35 mm (standard lens) | Side angle; hands hold the poster upright, artwork to camera; the liner leaves behind and to the right | 60 fps → 40 % | **Rack focus**: liner edge → artwork | Front key, rim light on the paper edges | Tap-to-focus during the take, or two takes cut together. 1 poster = 1 take. |
| S4 | 35 mm | Tracking: the poster enters from bottom right in an arc toward the wall | 24 fps | Follow | Same as S3 | Shoot the start of the movement so it **rhymes** with the end of the liner in S3 (same arc direction). |
| S5 | 100 mm macro | The top edge comes into contact with the wall; the shadow between paper and wall disappears | 120 fps, **ramp** 100 % → 35 % | On the edge | Very low raking light from above (the shadow line reads well) | Light from above makes the gap between paper and wall visible, so you see the contact happen. |
| S6 | 35 mm | Parallel tracking; the palm slides from the centre to the right | 24 fps | Poster | The torch follows the hand (an assistant) | Smooth for real: no bubbles, no creases on camera. |
| S7 | 35 mm | Static, the poster in shadow, the light goes out | 24 fps | – | Light off on camera (real switch) | Also record the **switch click** clean (foley). |
| S8 | 35 mm | Wide; the lamp clicks, the spot hits the poster; **slow dolly back** (or a slow digital zoom-out if the shot is locked off) | 24 fps | Poster | **A single source**: snooted spot on the poster, the room in natural falloff | A little "haze" (kettle steam behind the frame) gives volume to the beam 🔶. |
| S9 | 35 mm | 15° arc around the poster on a slider; foreground object out of focus | 24 fps | **Rack** object → poster | Spot + a highlight that glides across the paper (move the torch) | Show the **real finish** (matte or gloss): do not fake a shine. |
| S10 | 35 mm | Seen from behind, low; the person sits down; slow push-in | 24 fps | Poster | Spot only; rim light on the silhouette | Yourself or a friend (**written consent** if it's a third party). |
| S11 | – | Motion graphics on black | – | – | – | In CapCut / DaVinci Resolve with the **real logo** (PNG/SVG). |

### 3.3 Art direction
Charcoal / deep black palette, a single warm practical light (≈ 3200 K) against cool-neutral shadows 🔶. Minimal set: a wooden desk, a lamp, one object linked to the audience (headphones = music, controller = gamers, model car = car lovers). Pick **one per version**: never three at once. The only saturated colour in the frame is **the poster**.

## 5. Sound design timeline

Principle: **every sound = an action on screen**, recorded for real wherever possible (foley). Your phone, in a quiet room, 10–20 cm from the source. Record the **real peel** of your adhesive poster (5 takes, slow / fast), the fingernail on the edge, the palm on the paper, the paper against the wall, the lamp switch.

| Time | Layer | Description | Level / pan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00.00 | Sub | 40–45 Hz swell from silence, 0.9 s attack | −18 dB, centre |
| 0:00.00–1.00 | Texture | Very fine high shimmer (filtered noise 6–12 kHz) following the light sweep | −40 dB, L→R |
| 0:01.00 | Foley | **Fingernail tick** on the liner edge (dry, close) | −12 dB, slightly right |
| 0:01.05–1.35 | Foley | First adhesive crackles | −24 dB |
| 0:02.00–3.80 | **Foley (signature)** | **THE PEEL**: real recording stretched in time (not pitched down), + a low "tear" layer 80–300 Hz for weight | −14 dB, panned L→R with the movement |
| 0:02.00 | Music | Felt-piano note D3 | −18 dB |
| 0:03.65–4.35 | FX | Soft air whoosh (no cartoon "swoosh") on the match cut | −20 dB, L→R |
| 0:05.00 | Foley | Dull **contact** (paper + finger against the wall) + light sub 70 Hz | −10 dB |
| 0:06.00–7.00 | Foley | Palm on paper friction, in sync with the hand | −30 dB, follows the hand |
| 0:06.00 | Music | Piano note A3 | −20 dB |

## 6. Music direction

- **Character:** minimal, intimate, expensive. Felt piano (dampened), deep sub-bass, warm pad, granular textures. **No** "epic trailer", no corporate ukulele, no festival drop.
- **Tempo / key:** 120 BPM in **half-time** (felt at 60), **D minor**. One cut per beat = 12 frames.
- **Structure:** bar 1–2 (0–4 s): almost nothing, single notes · bar 3–4 (4–8 s): tension, then **total silence at 7.0 s** · bar 5–7 (8–14 s): full entry (pad + 3-note motif) · bar 8–9 (14–18 s): silence then **sonic logo**.
- **Sonic logo:** the peel + **A3 · D4 · F4**. Reuse it at the end of every video, every story and every WhatsApp voice note: that's how a brand becomes recognisable by ear.
- **Rights** ⚠️: no commercial songs, even trendy ones, in a **paid ad**. Options: (1) **original** music (a student composer, a credited exchange); (2) the **commercial** libraries of the platforms themselves (TikTok Commercial Music Library, Meta Sound Collection ✅), meant for brands; (3) AI music: check that your plan **explicitly** grants commercial use 🔶. The temp music in the animatic is a placeholder generated by code.

## 7. Editing and transitions

### 7.1 Cut list (24 fps)
Cuts at frames **0 · 24 · 48 · 96 · 120 · 144 · 168 · 192 · 240 · 288 · 336 · 432**: every one on a beat (multiples of 12).

### 7.2 Transitions (only these)
1. **Hard cuts** everywhere else. No zooms, glitches, flashes or sliding transitions.
2. **S3 → S4 match cut:** the curve of the liner leaving frame on the right = the arc of the poster coming in. Same direction and speed of movement.
3. **S6 → S7 → S8 dark to reveal:** the light goes out on camera (not a fade), 1 s of black, then a **hard cut** onto the impact + spot. A macro-to-wide move through darkness.
4. **S10 → S11:** cut to black on the beat at 14.0 s.

### 7.3 Speed
- S2: 120 fps conformed to 24 = **20 %**. S3: 60 fps → **40 %**.
- S5: **speed ramp** 100 % → 35 % over 4 frames, the moment of contact at frame 130.
- Everything else at real speed. Slow motion is a punctuation mark, not a style.

### 7.4 Grade
- **Rich, not crushed** blacks (≈ 2–4 %), soft highlight roll-off, light **fine grain** across the whole film (it also blends real and AI shots).
- ⚠️ **Protect the poster's colours:** grade the environment, not the artwork (mask / qualifier). Check against the print PDF. A poster that looks better in the ad than in real life will get returned.
- No heavy vignette, no HDR, no oversaturation.

### 7.5 Export
Master **2160 × 3840, 24 fps** (H.265 high bitrate, or ProRes if your software allows) + a **1080 × 1920 H.264** version for upload. Audio AAC 48 kHz, 320 kbps. The apps recompress: upload the best version they accept.

### 7.6 Versions to derive from the same shoot
- **15 s:** drop S4 and S10, and shorten S11 to 3 s.
- **6 s bumper:** S1 + S3 (peel) + S11 sonic logo.
- **"Swap" version:** same edit, same sound, another poster (your first original design for car lovers or gamers, with the matching foreground object).
